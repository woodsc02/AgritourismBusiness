What to expect

Tips for a great visit

What to wear
Closed-toe shoes are a must — this is a working farm. Dress for the weather and expect some mud after rain. Layers are smart in spring and fall.
Best time to visit
First thing in the morning for cooler temperatures and shorter lines. Peak hours are 11am – 2pm on fall Saturdays.
How long to allow
A full visit with a day pass takes 2.5 – 4 hours. If you're just doing one or two things, allow 60 – 90 minutes.
Payments
We accept cash and all major credit and debit cards. Apple Pay and Google Pay accepted at the ticket booth and food stand.
Food on site
Our hub has food and drink available. No outside food or coolers please — this keeps our grounds clean and our vendors viable.
Cell service
Service can be spotty in parts of the farm. Download tickets before you arrive. Free Wi-Fi available in the common hub.

FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need to buy tickets in advance?
No reservation is needed for general admission. Walk up any open day and buy at the gate. For groups of 20+, advance booking is required. For school field trips, book at least 2 weeks ahead.
Is the farm accessible for guests with disabilities?
Yes. The common hub, restrooms, and main paths are ADA-accessible. The wagon has an accessible boarding step. Some field areas involve uneven terrain — staff can advise on the best routes on arrival.
What happens if it rains?
We operate rain or shine in light weather. In the case of severe weather or dangerous conditions, we may close early — check our social media or call ahead if the forecast looks rough.
Can I bring a stroller?
Yes — paved paths connect the parking lot to the hub and main attraction areas. Field paths are packed gravel or dirt and are manageable with most strollers. The wagon can accommodate folding strollers.
Is there somewhere to eat on site?
Yes. Our common hub has a food and drink stand with seasonal items. Outside food and coolers are not permitted on the property.
Are pets allowed?
No — for the safety of our farm animals and other guests, pets are not permitted on the property.
