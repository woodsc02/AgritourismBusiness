Season guide

What to expect each season

Plan your visit around the rhythms of spring, summer, and fall so every trip feels effortless.

Spring

Weekends only, with blooming fields and baby animals leading the season.

Summer

Thursday through Sunday, with the full farm experience open for families.

Fall

Daily farm access and October evenings with the haunted maze.
