What to bring

Comfort and practicality

Closed-toe shoes, a light jacket, sunscreen, and a small bag for pumpkins or picked flowers.

What to expect

Real farm rhythms

This is a working farm. Expect gentle livestock noises, natural paths, and a calm seasonal pace.

Accessibility

Careful farm access

Main areas are accessible. Ask staff at arrival for the best route if mobility is a concern.

Group visits

Bring a group

Whether it’s a family reunion or a youth group, we help plan visits that keep everyone together and energized.
