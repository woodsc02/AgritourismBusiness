Explore the farm

Attractions to slow down and enjoy

Each attraction is designed around a real working livestock farm, with thoughtful seasonal experiences rather than flashy gimmicks.

Petting Zoo

A gentle gathering place

Spring through fall, the petting zoo brings kids close to goats, sheep, and rabbits with soft paths and quiet interactions.

Seasonal availability: spring–fall

Photo: Petting Zoo
Flower Field

U-pick blooms for every bouquet

May through July, families can walk among fragrant blooms and gather fresh flowers with clippers and wrapping included.

Seasonal availability: May–July

Photo: Flower Field
Apple Orchard

Harvest your own orchard apples

August through October, wander orchard rows and pick crisp apples for a true Idaho harvest experience.

Seasonal availability: August–October

Photo: Apple Orchard
Pumpkin Patch

Autumn fields of pumpkins

September through October, choose from a wide patch of pumpkins, gourds, and fall textures in a welcoming farm setting.

Seasonal availability: September–October

Photo: Pumpkin Patch
Hay Maze

Family-friendly maze adventure

Spring through fall, the hay maze invites exploration through winding paths and hidden farm discoveries.

Seasonal availability: spring–fall

Photo: Hay Maze
Haunted Maze

October evening adventure

A ticketed, October-only maze with gentle scares and seasonal storytelling for older kids and adults.

Seasonal availability: October only

Photo: Haunted Maze
