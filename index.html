Attractions overview

Six authentic farm experiences

From waking baby animals to autumn harvest paths, each attraction is designed for a genuine family farm day.

Petting Zoo

Meet goats, sheep, rabbits, and friendly livestock in a shaded pens area that feels warm and calm.

Flower Field

U-pick blooms, gentle meadow paths, and a bright seasonal scene from April through July.

Apple Orchard

August to October harvests let families pick crisp apples straight from the tree.

Pumpkin Patch

A wide field of pumpkins, gourds, and fall textures for a classic Idaho autumn day.

Hay Maze

Navigate the maze route on fresh hay paths made for exploring with kids.

Haunted Maze

October-only evenings with a gently spooky walk for older kids and families.
