Attractions overview

Six authentic farm experiences

From baby animals and flower picking to pumpkins and evening mazes, every visit feels grounded and genuine.

Petting Zoo

Meet goats, sheep, rabbits, and friendly livestock in a shaded meadow with calm, guided interactions.

Flower Field

Pick seasonal bouquets in a quiet field, with clippers and wrapping included for easy take-home blooms.

Apple Orchard

Harvest crisp apples straight from the tree during late summer and early fall.

Pumpkin Patch

Browse a wide patch of pumpkins, gourds, and harvest textures for fall photos and family memories.

Hay Maze

Explore winding paths in our seasonal hay maze that’s designed for families with curiosity.

Haunted Maze

October nights bring a gently spooky maze for older kids and adults seeking a seasonal adventure.
