FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is food included in the day pass?
No — food and drink from our hub vendors are priced separately. The day pass covers all attractions and u-pick fields only.
If I start paying per attraction, can I switch to a day pass mid-visit?
Absolutely. If you find yourself wanting to do more, stop by the ticket booth and we'll upgrade you — whatever you've already paid will be credited toward the day pass price.
Can I upgrade from a day pass to a season pass on the same visit?
Yes — we'll credit your day pass purchase toward a season pass if you decide to upgrade on the same day.
Are all attractions open every day?
Availability varies by season. The petting zoo and wagon rides run spring through fall. The pumpkin patch and hay maze open in September. Check our attractions page for what's currently open.
Are pets allowed?
No — for the safety of our farm animals and other guests, pets are not permitted on the property.
