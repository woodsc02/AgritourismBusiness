About Us

A working farm opened to families

HollowCreek began as a livestock operation in Rexburg. Today, we share farm life through experiences that feel honest, warm, and welcoming.

Origin story

From livestock roots to family farm weekends

We started with animals, crops, and a real working farmstead in Rexburg. The visitor side grew naturally as neighbors asked to see the fields, meet the livestock, and enjoy the working rhythms of the land.

Brand values

Authenticity, affordability, and community

Our farm prioritizes honest experiences, affordable access, repeat visits that feel like membership, and a welcoming place for families to connect with real agriculture.

Family first

We run HollowCreek as a family team. Our daily life is the farm: feeding animals, tending fields, and sharing that life with guests.

Our farm team

From caretakers to barn crews, our team helps families feel comfortable and connected on every visit.
