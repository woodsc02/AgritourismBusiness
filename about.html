About Us

A working farm opened to families

HollowCreek began with livestock and harvest, and now welcomes guests into the rhythms of real farm life.

Origin story

From livestock roots to family weekends

We started with animals, crops, and working fields. Visitors asked to see the farm, and HollowCreek became a place to share authentic agricultural life.

Brand values

Authenticity, affordability, community

We prioritize honest, affordable experiences, repeat farm access, and a welcoming atmosphere for families.

Family first

HollowCreek is run as a family farm. Every visit is supported by people who live the work and share the land.

Farm team

From caretakers to barn crew, our team helps guests feel comfortable, informed, and connected with farm life.
